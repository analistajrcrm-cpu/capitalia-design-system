Single-choice field for known lists (etapa, tipo de lote, medio de contacto).

```jsx
<Select label="Etapa de interés" options={["Etapa 1 — Recoleta","Etapa 2","Etapa 3"]} />
```

- Use for 4+ options; below that use Radio so every option is visible.
- The caret is a text glyph, matching the brand's typographic arrows.
