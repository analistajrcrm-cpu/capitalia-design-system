The standard action. Flat fill, fully rounded, no shadow, no gradient.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg" iconRight={<Icon name="arrow-right" size={18}/>}>Agendar visita</Button>
```

- One `primary` (orange) per view; `secondary` (plum) for the second action; `outline`/`ghost` for tertiary; `inverse` on plum or photography.
- Hover darkens the fill, press darkens again. Nothing scales, lifts, or glows.
- `shape="petal"` only when the button is the brand moment on the page (hero CTA on a poster).
