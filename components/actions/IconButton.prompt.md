Icon-only control for toolbars, carousels and card overflow menus.

```jsx
<IconButton label="Siguiente" variant="outline"><Icon name="arrow-right" /></IconButton>
```

- `inverse` when it sits on plum or a photo; `accent` only for a single dominant action (play, next).
- Hit area is 32/40/48px — never smaller than 40px on touch surfaces.
