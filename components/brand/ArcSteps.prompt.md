Stacks short benefit statements under sweeping hairline arcs with numbered circles — a deck layout, not a UI list.

```jsx
<ArcSteps tone="inverse" items={["Plusvalía sostenida","Áreas verdes en cada etapa","Vialidades y servicios listos"]} />
```

- Built for the plum slide; on cream pass `tone="default"`.
- Keep each item to one sentence. Beyond five steps the arcs crowd — split the slide instead.
