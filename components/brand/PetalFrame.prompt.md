Crops an image into a brand shape. This — not a rectangle with rounded corners — is how Capitalia presents people and places.

```jsx
<PetalFrame src="../../assets/img/parque-render.png" shape="petal" point="br" ratio="4 / 5" />
```

- `petal` for hero imagery and portraits, `circle` for tight portrait crops, `arc` for photo bands that meet a panel edge, `leaf` sparingly.
- Point the squared corner toward the copy it belongs to, and vary the point between adjacent frames so masks don't rhyme.
- Photography is warm, sunlit, people-first: families, plazas, planted streets. Never place a mask over another mask.
