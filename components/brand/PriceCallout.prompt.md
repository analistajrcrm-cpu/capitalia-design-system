Prices are the loudest thing on a sales piece: bold Spanish lead-in, orange figure, small unit.

```jsx
<PriceCallout label="Mensualidades desde" amount="$2,900" unit="mxn" note="Lotes desde 140 m²" />
```

- Only the figure is orange. Never colour the label or the unit.
- One callout per composition; pair with a PetalTag carrying the URL.
